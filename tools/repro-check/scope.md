# Scope: where your issue lives, and the rules of the room

## Where your issue lives

Only issues in the course's Path Review repository are in scope this week:

- Repo: `codepath/pathreview-ai301-fa26-s1`

The package must belong to the issue selected in Unit 1 (or an instructor-routed house issue). Eval mode ignores this scope.

## Path Review house rules

A classmate's claim does not block mine. I post my own claim before reproducing, even on a shared issue. I post my own reproduction from my environment, with my own steps and artifacts, instead of replying "same as above." Credit attaches to the work posted, not to being first.
