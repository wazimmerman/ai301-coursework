# Rubric: is this plan ready to post and build from?

## Checks

| Check | Evidence | Pass condition | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Diagnosis follows reproduction | Candidate plan's diagnosis and draft comment compared with the issue's expected behavior and the repro-evidence steps, failures, controls, and artifacts | The proposed explanation accounts for the reproduced failure and relevant controls without contradicting them or dismissing decisive evidence. A hypothesis is allowed when labelled and paired with a concrete verification step. | required |
| Change reaches the cause | Plan's approach compared with the reproduced failure boundary and diagnosis | The proposed intervention reaches the demonstrated or explicitly testable cause rather than hiding the symptom, changing unrelated behavior, or relying on a thread claim contradicted by the reproduction. | required |
| One bounded change | Plan's scope, exclusions, files or areas, and approach read against the issue and maintainer direction | The work is one coherent fix for this issue, with an identifiable boundary; supporting tests and necessary docs belong to that fix. No unrelated rewrite, feature, or broad cleanup is bundled in. Scope can be implicit in a precise small change; a separate non-goals heading is not required. | required |
| Executable approach | Plan's named files or functional areas, concrete operations, ordering, and dependencies | A contributor can start the proposed change without guessing the affected component or inventing the core mechanism. Exact filenames or numbered steps are unnecessary when an unambiguous change site and operation are given. Exploration alone, vague promises, and unexplained placeholders do not pass. | required |
| Observable regression proof | Plan's test steps and expected results compared with the accepted repro's triggering inputs, environment, failure, and controls | At least one named or clearly referenced test replays the reproduced failure and states the observable result that distinguishes the fix from the broken behavior. Preserve relevant controls or adjacent shared paths when identified. A broad suite alone, or merely saying tests pass or it works, does not prove the regression. | required |
| Claims and unknowns are honest | Diagnosis, risks, unknowns, deviations, and draft comment compared with supplied evidence | Claims do not overstate what the evidence proves, promise an unverified result or completion date, or conceal a known conflict or deviation. Material uncertainties are acknowledged with a verification or decision path. A small well-supported fix need not invent risks or include a ritual unknowns section. | required |
| Thread and repository respected | Draft plan comment and plan compared with thread highlights, repo-facts contribution policy, template asks relevant to the plan, and live voice guide | The comment conveys this package's own diagnosis, bounded change, and verification, and honors relevant maintainer direction and stated contribution or AI-use policy. It does not piggyback, ignore a stated prerequisite, or contradict a request. Do not invent missing policies, treat selective review as a blanket ban, or require a plan comment to repeat every field of a bug-report template. | required |

## Verdict rule

Return accept (ready to post and build from) only when every required check passes. Return reject (hold) if any required check fails or is unclear. Missing decisive evidence is unclear; an explicit contradiction is fail. Preferred checks, if later added, never change the verdict. A terse package can pass; length, polish, section count, and confidence do not substitute for evidence.
